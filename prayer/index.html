一、感恩节福音爱宴与福音果子的存留

Thanksgiving Gospel Gathering and Lasting Fruit

路加福音 15:24；哥林多后书 12:14

感谢主祝福10月11日的感恩节福音爱宴，借着《两个儿子》的信息显明父神寻找失丧者的心。求主记念圣徒在邀请、接待、饭食和牧养上的劳苦，使福音朋友认识父神的爱，接受基督，进入神的话与稳定的教会生活；也坚固近期得救、受浸的人。

We thank the Lord for the October 11 Thanksgiving gospel gathering and the message “The Two Sons,” revealing the Father's heart to seek the lost. May the Lord remember the saints' labor in invitation, hospitality, meals, and shepherding; lead our friends to know His love, receive Christ, and enter into His Word and a steady church life; and strengthen those recently saved and baptized.

二、社区、校园福音与持续牧养

Community and Campus Gospel and Shepherding

帖撒罗尼迦前书 2:8

感谢主带领圣徒在约克大学分发福音单张、摆桌、举行福音爱宴，并持续接触社区居民。求主使福音工作从广泛撒种进入具体牧养，兴起圣徒一对一联络、探访、陪读圣经、接送和陪同聚会，也使青年圣徒学习牧养别人。

We thank the Lord for gospel outreach at York University and in the community. May our work advance from broad sowing to personal shepherding through individual contact, visits, Bible reading, transportation, and fellowship. May young believers learn to care for others.

三、为圣徒、家庭与青年人祷告

Saints, Households, and Young People

马太福音 18:18

求主赐圣徒及家庭平安、恩典与供应。保守刚进入大学生活的青年人及家中的青少年，使他们不受世界、网络和忙碌霸占，反而爱主、读经、祷告，并有属灵同伴。求主使教会学习同心合意地祷告。

May the Lord grant the saints and their households peace, grace, and supply. Preserve university students and teenagers from worldly distractions; draw them to love the Lord, read the Bible, pray, and pursue with spiritual companions. Teach the church to pray in one accord.

四、为主在全地的行动祷告

The Lord's Move Throughout the Earth

Jonathan弟兄从非洲回来，求主膏抹他的交通，使圣徒看见基督身体的广大与主在全地的行动。求主坚固非洲的众教会、圣徒、同工与福音工作，也扩大我们的祷告度量，激励青年人奉献并服事主。

As Jonathan returns from Africa, may the Lord anoint his sharing so that the saints see the breadth of Christ's Body and His move on earth. Strengthen the churches, saints, co-workers, and gospel work in Africa, enlarge our prayer, and inspire young believers to consecrate themselves.
