THANKSGIVING & PRAISE

感谢与赞美

我们来到主面前，首先不是提出我们的需要，乃是向主献上感谢和赞美。

感谢主把 Jonathan 弟兄平安带回到我们中间。感谢主保守他在非洲各地的行程、身体和服事，也借着他的交通，使我们看见主的工作不只在多伦多，也在许多地方继续往前。

特别为 9 月 25 日的福音聚集感谢赞美主。主把许多福音朋友和新人带到我们中间，也使圣徒在饭食、接待、见证、信息和翻译上同心配搭。这不是我们自己作成的，乃是主在我们中间作了奇妙的事。

若我们没有看见主已经作的，又怎能摸着主今天的旨意，并为祂的旨意争战祷告？

THE BURDEN OF PRAYER

本周祷告的中心

“凡你们在地上所捆绑的，在天上也要捆绑；凡你们在地上所释放的，在天上也要释放。” — 马太福音 18:18

教会的祷告不是仅仅把人的需要带到神面前，乃是先寻求神的旨意，看见神正在作什么，然后在地上与天上的行动配合。

主啊，你已经开始的工作，下一步要怎样往前？你所要得着的人是谁？你所要建造的家在哪里？愿我们在身体里同心合意地与你配合。

01
GOSPEL & SHEPHERDING

为福音从“广泛接触”进入“持续牧养”祷告

九月份密集的校园福音、福音 BBQ、主日福音爱宴和中秋福音聚集，使我们接触到许多学生和福音朋友。现在需要从广泛接触往前，进入持续接触、邀请和牧养。

求主不要让所得着的名字停留在名单里，使我们一个一个地祷告、一个一个地接触、一个一个地陪伴。

也为 York University 摆桌申请、Finch West Subway Station 的福音行动，以及刚刚开始的 Humber College 福音接触祷告。求主预备平安之子，并兴起年轻弟兄姊妹成为传福音、牧养人并尽功用的人。

02
THE BODY OF CHRIST

为主在各地的行动和基督身体的交通祷告

感谢主带领 Jonathan 弟兄回来。求主借着他的交通，使我们不只关心西区自己的需要，更看见主在全地的行动。

无论主在非洲、多伦多或其他地方所赐下的恩典，都不是只为一个地方，乃是为着基督整个身体。愿主扩大我们的度量，使一地所得的恩典成为众召会共同的享受。

求主使我们不只为自己的聚会、人数和需要祷告，更为着神旨意在全地的通行和基督身体的建造祷告。

03
SAINTS, FAMILIES & NEXT GENERATION

为圣徒、家庭和下一代祷告

求主在西区不仅得着个人，更得着一个家、一个家。

特别为青少年、刚进入大学的年轻人、在职弟兄姊妹以及年轻家庭祷告。求主保守年轻人在新的校园环境中与主有个人的关系，并继续联于教会生活。

也求主加强在职圣徒和年轻家庭，使他们在工作、家庭和教会生活中经历基督，并逐渐成为能够接待人、牧养人、托住年轻人、承担教会生活的家。

— Matthew 18:19
THANKSGIVING & PRAISE

Thanksgiving and Praise

As we come before the Lord, we begin not with our needs, but with thanksgiving and praise.

We thank the Lord for bringing brother Jonathan safely back among us. We thank Him for preserving Jonathan’s travels, health, and service in Africa, and for enlarging our view to see that the Lord is moving not only in Toronto but also in many places.

We especially thank and praise the Lord for the gospel gathering on September 25. The Lord brought gospel friends and new ones among us and enabled the saints to coordinate in food service, receiving, testimonies, the message, and translation. This was not merely something we accomplished; it was the Lord’s wonderful work among us.

If we do not see what the Lord has already done, how can we touch His present will and pray fighting prayers for it?

THE BURDEN OF PRAYER

The Central Burden This Week

“Whatever you bind on the earth shall have been bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on the earth shall have been loosed in heaven.” — Matthew 18:18

The prayer of the church is not merely to bring human needs before God. It is first to seek God’s will, see what He is doing, and then cooperate on earth with the heavenly action.

Lord, how would You carry forward what You have begun? Whom do You desire to gain? Which households do You desire to build up? Make us one in the Body to cooperate with You in prayer.

01
GOSPEL & SHEPHERDING

Pray for the Gospel Work to Advance from Broad Contact to Continued Shepherding

Through the intensive campus gospel work, gospel BBQs, Lord’s Day gospel love feast, and Mid-Autumn gospel gathering in September, we contacted many students and gospel friends. We now need to advance from broad contact into continued contact, invitation, and shepherding.

May the Lord not allow the names we have gained to remain merely on a list. Cause us to pray for them one by one, contact them one by one, and accompany them one by one.

We also pray for the York University table application, the gospel action at Finch West Subway Station, and the newly begun contact at Humber College. May the Lord prepare sons of peace and raise up younger brothers and sisters to preach the gospel, shepherd people, and function.

02
THE BODY OF CHRIST

Pray for the Lord’s Move in Many Places and the Fellowship of the Body of Christ

We thank the Lord for bringing brother Jonathan back. Through his fellowship, may the Lord cause us to care not only for the needs of the West Area but also to see His move throughout the earth.

The grace the Lord gives in Africa, Toronto, or any other place is not merely for one locality but for the entire Body of Christ. May He enlarge our capacity so that the grace received in one place becomes the common enjoyment of the churches.

May the Lord lead us to pray not merely for our own meetings, numbers, and needs, but for the carrying out of God’s will on earth and the building up of the Body of Christ.

03
SAINTS, FAMILIES & NEXT GENERATION

Pray for the Saints, Families, and the Next Generation

May the Lord gain not only individuals in the West Area, but household after household.

We especially pray for the young people, those beginning university, working saints, and young families. Preserve the young people in a personal relationship with You and keep them connected to the church life.

Strengthen the working saints and young families to experience Christ in work, family, and church life, and gradually become homes that can receive and shepherd people, support the young ones, and bear responsibility in the church life.
